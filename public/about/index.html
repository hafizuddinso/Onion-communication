Research prototype

MSc research · ETU “LETI” · 2026

Secure communication over onion routing.

Onion Communication was developed as part of Hafizuddin’s Master’s thesis, Framework for Safe Communication in the Non-Indexed Web (Hidden Web). The prototype brings together a communication interface, Tor onion-service access and application-layer cryptographic experiments.

It is a research and educational system, not a production messenger. The implementation provides a practical baseline for studying anonymous communication, cryptographic key establishment, system resilience and future post-quantum migration.
